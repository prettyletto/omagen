# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive
community. Participate in good faith, assume positive intent, explain
disagreements clearly, and focus criticism on ideas and behavior rather than
people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information without consent are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
security contact listed in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not create a public
issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove content, restrict
participation, or take other action appropriate to protect the community.
